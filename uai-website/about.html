About United Agro Innovation

Empowering Entrepreneurs, Transforming Industries

Who We Are

UAI is a multi-sector innovation, investment, entrepreneurship, agriculture, technology, and project development organization dedicated to African excellence and global impact.

Our Vision

To be the premier global hub for sustainable innovation and high-impact investments that redefine Africa's role in the global economy.

Our Mission

To identify, develop, and scale innovative projects across agriculture, technology, and commerce through collaborative investment and strategic leadership.
