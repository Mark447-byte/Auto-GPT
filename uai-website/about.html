About United Agro Innovation

Forging the path of sustainable African industrialization since 2020.

Who We Are

United Agro Innovation (UAI) is a sophisticated multi-sector innovation, investment, and project development engine. We operate at the intersection of traditional industry and digital transformation, focusing on sectors that provide the highest leverage for structural change in Africa.

Our model is built on the concept of "Collective Capitalization"—pooling the resources, expertise, and ambitions of African professionals and global investors to build world-class enterprises. From smart-farming clusters in the hinterlands to fintech-driven trade corridors, UAI is the catalyst for the next generation of African success stories.

Our Vision

To be the premier global hub for sustainable innovation and high-impact investments that redefine Africa's role in the global economy by 2030.

Our Mission

To identify, de-risk, and scale innovative projects across agriculture, technology, and renewable energy through strategic leadership and transparent governance.

Investor Ready

We maintain a rigorous institutional-grade investment framework to ensure maximum transparency and value for our stakeholders.
